**Prior art consulted** (documentation prose):
- OpenSlide's public format page, https://openslide.org/formats/mirax/ (CC-BY-SA-4.0 text): the Index.dat layout (version string, SLIDE_ID, the hierarchical and non-hierarchical root pointers, linked lists of data pages whose first page is empty, 4-integer hierarchical items and 5-integer non-hierarchical items), the data-file header, the 9-byte camera-position records and their flag, the version-2.2 DEFLATE-compressed position table under `StitchingIntensityLayer`, which non-hierarchical records hold the label, macro and thumbnail images.
- Benjamin Gilbert, "Introduction to MIRAX/MRXS", openslide-users mailing list, 2012-07-24, https://lists.andrew.cmu.edu/pipermail/openslide-users/2012-July/000373.html (a prose description): camera photos split into `CameraImageDivisionsPerSide` images per side; each higher level concatenates 2 × 2 images of the level below and halves them, whatever the overlaps; image index `y · IMAGENUMBER_X + x` with x, y multiples of the level's step; images of higher levels hold several camera positions ("subtiles"), each placed at its camera's level-0 position divided by 2^level; saved (downsampled) slides drop levels and raise `IMAGE_CONCAT_FACTOR` of the new lowest level.

**Black-box oracle:** OpenSlide 4.0.1 through openslide-python 1.4.6 (`oracle/.venv`), run on the corpus files: `level_dimensions`, `properties` (`openslide.bounds-*`, `mpp`, `background-color`) and `read_region` pixels (RGBA). Nothing from its source.

**Method** (a Python walker and renderer in the session scratchpad, then comparisons with OpenSlide's regions):
- `Slidedat.ini`: UTF-8 with a byte-order mark, CRLF, `[SECTION]` and `KEY = VALUE`. `[HIERARCHICAL]` names the index file, `HIER_*` trees (`Slide zoom level`: one value per pyramid level, each with its own section: `MICROMETER_PER_PIXEL_X/Y`, `OVERLAP_X/Y`, `IMAGE_CONCAT_FACTOR`, `IMAGE_FORMAT`, `IMAGE_FILL_COLOR_BGR`, `DIGITIZER_WIDTH/HEIGHT`; `Slide filter level`: one value per filter with `FILTER_NAME`, `COLOR_R/G/B`, `STORING_CHANNEL_NUMBER`, `EXPOSURE_TIME`) and `NONHIER_*` trees; `[DATAFILE]` lists `FILE_0 … FILE_n`.
- Index.dat: confirmed the documented layout on all seven files; a root entry of 0 is an empty record (the version-2 fluorescence files write 0 for the empty filter and scan-info records). Hierarchical items of zoom level k: index, offset, length, file number; the images decode to `DIGITIZER_WIDTH × DIGITIZER_HEIGHT` at every level.
- Level steps: the image indices of level k are multiples of 2^(sum of IMAGE_CONCAT_FACTOR of levels 0..k): 1, 2, 4, … in the unsaved files (factor 0 at level 0, 1 above), 2, 4, … in the 1/2 save (factor 1 at level 0) and 16, 32, … in the 1/16 save (factor 4 at level 0). Micrometres per pixel double from level to level in every file.
- Camera positions (`VIMSLIDE_POSITION_BUFFER`/`default`, 9 bytes per camera, `IMAGENUMBER_X/div × IMAGENUMBER_Y/div` records, row-major) are in the pixels of the file's level 0 (the 1/16 save's are 1/16 of the unsaved slide's). Flag 1 marks the cameras whose images are stored (64 of 8 952 in `fluorescence-2`, 2 228 of 21 472 in the 1/16 save); the others hold 0 or garbage.
- Placement at level 0 (step s ≤ div): image (x, y) is at camera (x div d, y div d)'s position plus ((x mod d)/s · w, (y mod d)/s · h). Rendering every stored image that way, in raster order of its grid cell with later images over earlier ones, equals OpenSlide's level-0 regions sample for sample on both fluorescence files and both saves (JPEG decoded with libjpeg in both).
- Higher levels: positions divided by 2^level are fractional. OpenSlide's regions there are bilinear interpolations of the stored images at those fractional offsets: an interior window predicted by bilinear sampling differs by ≤ 0.8 grey levels, by nearest-pixel placement by up to 34. Seams: where two images of one camera meet at a half pixel, OpenSlide's pixel is the mean of the two edge rows with full opacity, which is what compositing each image with transparent outside (bilinear taps outside the image transparent) under a "saturate" rule (a source adds only as much as the destination still lacks) gives, painting in reverse raster order (so the later image wins where photos overlap fully).
- Subtiles (step s > d): an image holds (s/d)² camera positions, each `w·d/s × h·d/s` pixels, placed at its own camera's position / 2^level; cameras beyond `IMAGENUMBER_X/d` columns are skipped (not wrapped into the next row), as are cameras without stored images. Where a subtile's source rectangle starts at a fractional pixel, OpenSlide's values are explained (≤ 1.2 grey levels) by first resampling the subtile into its own `ceil(w·d/s) × ceil(h·d/s)` surface at that fractional offset, then placing that surface bilinearly; a single resampling differs by up to 30. Paint order: raster order of each subtile's first grid cell.
- With these rules the model agrees with OpenSlide at every level of all four files checked so far to within 3.3 grey levels at the 99.9th percentile (a handful of edge pixels at the coarsest level of `fluorescence-1` differ by up to 31).
- Level-0 size: OpenSlide's is `cameras_x · (photo_w − o) + o` (truncated), photo_w = `w · d / s0`, with o = 96 in the unsaved files, 30 in the 1/2 save and ≈ 0.5 in the 1/16 save, i.e. `round(OVERLAP_X of level 0) / s0` in all three kinds (the saved files' OVERLAP_X is already at their own scale, so this is OpenSlide's convention, not a physical overlap). Level k size = floor(level-0 size / 2^k). We use the same sizes so that coordinates agree with OpenSlide-based tools.
- Fluorescence: `SLIDE_TYPE_FLUORESCENCE`, three filters with `STORING_CHANNEL_NUMBER` 0, 1, 2 in one 3-component JPEG. In `fluorescence-1` the decoded red component shows nuclei (round, bright, dark background), and the `UV` filter has storing channel 2; the `Rhodamine` filter (storing channel 0) is the blue component. So storing channel k is decoded component 2 − k (B, G, R order, as `IMAGE_FILL_COLOR_BGR`'s name says). OpenSlide returns the composite RGB (Rhodamine shown in blue); we return one channel per filter.
- `SLIDE_CREATIONDATETIME` is `DD/MM/YYYY hh:mm:ss`: `29/12/2009` and `20/02/2008` settle the order.
