# Corpus sweep, October 2026

`scripts/sweep/sweep.py` runs the release binary over development files and runs each command twice. It records exit codes, peak memory, run time and output hashes, and flags panics, commands killed at the 3 GiB or 120-second limit, errors without a hint, exports that were not verified, and output that differs between the two runs. Held-out files are skipped.

## First run

The first run, on commit `0ffcf3df` with three jobs, covered 174 development files (1,688 command pairs). It found four problems:

| file | command | finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `mtbls773-001-blank-start` (mzML) | `export --format mzml` | panic, and the panic message changed between runs |
| `mtbls773-001-blank-start` (mzML) | `export --format parquet --spectra`, `arrow` | exit 1 with a generic hint |
| `openslide-zeiss-5-cropped` | `export --format ome-zarr --pyramid none` | killed above 3 GiB of RSS in one of the two runs |
| `zenodo10577621-Young-mouse` | `stats` on the whole 190,309 × 69,378 plane | over 120 s with one thread |

## What was fixed

**Optical spectra in mzML.** The mzML file holds 2,622 mass spectra and 10,501 spectra from a PDA detector. The PDA spectra have a wavelength array and no m/z array. The reader returned them as mass spectra with no m/z values, and the mzML writer panicked when it looked for the base peak. Now `spectrum` refuses an optical spectrum with exit 6 and a hint, and the mzML export leaves the 10,501 PDA spectra out, writes the 2,622 mass spectra and the three chromatograms, and reports `spectra_skipped: 10501` with a warning. `info`, `scans` and `analyze chromatogram` give the same output as before. The mzML exports of `pwiz-waters-qc-lcms2-2-23-268-1-1-mzml` and `pwiz-agilent-tof-sulfas-mzml`, which also hold PDA spectra, panicked the same way and now leave out 8 of 10 and 520 of 732 spectra. The Parquet and Arrow spectra exports now stop with exit 6 and the reader's hint instead of exit 1.

**OME-Zarr memory.** The in-memory OME-Zarr writer took planes up to 4 GiB and held each plane several times over. Planes larger than 256 MiB now go through the streaming writer. Peak RSS measured with `/usr/bin/time -l`, one thread:

| file | export | before | after |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `openslide-zeiss-5-cropped` | `--pyramid none` | 2.92 GB | 0.90 GB |
| `openslide-zeiss-5-cropped` | `--pyramid mean` | 3.06 GB | 1.06 GB |
| `ome-qptiff-hande-compressed-scan1` | `--pyramid none` | 4.64 GB | 1.03 GB |

The stores are byte-identical before and after (SHA-256 of every file), for these three exports and for `openslide-philips-1` and `ome-subresolutions-retina-large`, which already used the streaming writer. `crates/openreadout-bench/tests/memory_ceiling.rs` now exports one 512 MiB synthetic plane and checks that the peak heap stays within one 256 MiB block plus 32 MiB. With the old 4 GiB cutoff that export peaked at 1,026 MiB.

## After the fixes

A run over the two `mtbls773-001-blank-start` entries (the Thermo `.raw` and its mzML) and `pyteomics-tiny-pwiz` found nothing: 51 command pairs, exit 0 or 6 throughout.

## Still open

- The Parquet and Arrow spectra exports of `mtbls773-001-blank-start` stop at the first optical spectrum instead of leaving the optical spectra out as the mzML export does.
- `stats` over a whole 13-gigapixel plane takes longer than 120 s on one thread. That is expected work rather than a bug, so the sweep will keep reporting it until it uses a region or a pyramid level on such files.
