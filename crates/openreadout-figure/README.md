# openreadout-figure

Vector figures for OpenReadout: a small scene of paths, text and raster images, built in the editorial OpenReadout look (paper and ink, a warm red accent, quiet axes, direct labels), and rendered in-process to PNG, JPEG or SVG. Text uses the embedded IBM Plex Sans and Mono fonts with their real metrics and kerning. On top of the scene sit scales with nice ticks, linear and log axes, plot panels with lines, areas, bands, markers, bars and heat maps, small multiples, and a label placer that keeps labels apart.

This crate is part of [OpenReadout](https://github.com/openreadout/openreadout), which reads raw lab-instrument files without vendor software. Licensed MIT OR Apache-2.0. The embedded IBM Plex fonts are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`fonts/OFL.txt`).
