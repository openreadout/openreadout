Sample files of the OpenReadout browser demo

These files come from OpenReadout's public test corpus (corpus/manifest.toml in the
OpenReadout repository), unchanged apart from their names. Each keeps its own licence.
The demo page credits them next to each sample.

CMU-1-Small-Region.svs
  Aperio SVS slide scan. Carnegie Mellon University, OpenSlide test data.
  https://openslide.cs.cmu.edu/download/openslide-testdata/Aperio/
  Licence: CC0-1.0 (public domain dedication).

mouse-intestine.czi
  Zeiss LSM 710 confocal image (2_mouse_04_03.czi). Katherine Deets and Russell Vance,
  "Immunofluorescence images: Inflammasome activation leads to cDC1-independent
  cross-priming of CD8 T cells by epithelial cell derived antigen".
  https://zenodo.org/records/5823010
  Licence: CC0-1.0.

phage-defence.pda
  SoftMax Pro 5 document (120308b_2.pda). Dominik Refardt, Tobias Bergmiller and Rolf
  Kümmerli, "Data from: Altruism can evolve when relatedness is low: evidence from
  bacteria committing suicide upon phage infection", doi:10.5061/dryad.b1q2n.
  https://zenodo.org/records/4976789
  Licence: CC0-1.0.

agilent-gcms.cdf
  Agilent GC-MS run in AIA/ANDI netCDF. cheminfo/netcdf-gcms test data
  (commit ee7e2c0, src/__tests__/data/agilent-gcms.cdf).
  https://github.com/cheminfo/netcdf-gcms
  Licence: MIT. Copyright (c) 2016 cheminfo.

17o05028_ic_steps.abf
  Axon ABF whole-cell recording. pyABF sample data (commit 3ad9cd6, data/abfs).
  https://github.com/swharden/pyABF
  Licence: MIT. Copyright (c) 2018 Scott W Harden.

test_comp_example.fcs
  Flow cytometry FCS file. FlowKit test data (commit f215904, data/test_comp_example.fcs).
  https://github.com/whitews/FlowKit
  Licence: BSD-3-Clause. Copyright (c) 2018, Scott White. All rights reserved.

limonene-13C.jdf
  JEOL Delta 13C NMR of limonene (Limonene_7020ug200uL_CDCl3_13CNMR_400MHz_Jeol.jdf).
  Noura Rayya, nmrXiv study S200 (project P33 CENAPTNMR), doi:10.57992/nmrxiv.p33.s200.
  https://nmrxiv.org/sample/S200
  Licence: CC0-1.0.

BioRad_qPCR_melt.rdml
  Bio-Rad CFX run in RDML. RDML R package (commit a898512, inst/extdata).
  https://github.com/kablag/RDML
  Licence: MIT. Copyright (c) 2019 Konstantin A. Blagodatskikh.

Beeswax_Burgos_Spain.SPA
  Thermo OMNIC ATR-FTIR spectrum. Michael B. Toffolo, "FTIR spectral library of the major
  components of archaeological sediments", doi:10.5281/zenodo.11093587.
  https://zenodo.org/records/14170891
  Licence: CC-BY-4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
